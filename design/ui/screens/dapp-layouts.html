Three ways to frame the bridge card

Every option has the same core: amount and token on top, the pair (Ethereum → PulseChain, with a flip button) built into the two boxes, an optional "send to another address", and one button. They differ in how they show a transfer in flight, how long it takes, and your past activity. Numbers are placeholders.

Card, then a tracker and activity below

One column. The card is the page. Anything in flight shows as a step bar under it; past transfers follow as a short list.
