The fee drawers, opening and closing

Live: tap the hand (tip), the gas can or the clock. Tapping the open one, or another one, closes or swaps it. No bounce, no overshoot; everything eases straight into place.

1.2488
  • Open, about 0.28 s in all: the panel grows to its exact height in 0.2 s with a quick ease-out. The orange 3-unit line (the deck) draws across its top from under the icon you tapped, and the content fades in and drops 4 px into place, starting 0.06 s in.
  • Close, 0.12 s: faster than opening, with an ease-in. Content and line go first, then the height collapses. Nothing hangs around.
  • Swap (another icon while one is open): the content cross-fades and the line redraws from the new icon; the height follows the new content.
  • The tapped icon's pill lights at the same time, so you see what you opened.
  • Reduced motion: it simply appears and disappears.
