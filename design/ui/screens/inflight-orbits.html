Every transfer in flight, at once

One loop joins the two chains: the top lane runs from the chain on the left to the chain on the right, the bottom lane back. Each lane is the four steps of a transfer. Each planet circling a step is one transfer waiting at it. Its colour is its token; a core marks $1,000 or more and a ring $10,000 or more. When a step completes, its dot travels to the next. At rest each step's planets crowd into a tilted orbit, seen in depth. Hover a step to open its orbit; hover a planet (tap on a phone) to stop it and read the transfer.

  • Orbits use the design language's one ring radius, multiplied: the first orbit holds 6 planets, each further orbit 6 more. Every planet eases toward its place, so moves, arrivals and re-spacing glide.
  • Delivered transfers leave through the right end and the count above ticks up.
  • With reduced motion, dots sit still on their rings; hover and tap work the same.
