Every transfer in flight, at once

The track is the four steps of a transfer. Each dot circling a step is one transfer waiting at it; bigger dots carry more value. When a step completes, its dot travels to the next. Hover a dot (tap on a phone) to stop that step's orbit and read the transfer.

  • Rings use the design language's one ring radius, multiplied: the first ring holds 6 dots, each further ring 6 more.
  • Delivered transfers leave through the right end and the count above ticks up.
  • With reduced motion, dots sit still on their rings; hover and tap work the same.
