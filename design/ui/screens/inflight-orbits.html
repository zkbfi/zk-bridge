Every transfer in flight, at once

One loop joins the two chains: the top lane runs from the chain on the left to the chain on the right, the bottom lane back. Each lane is the three steps of a transfer: sent, final on its chain, and proof ready (a valid proof is on the board; posting it on chain is part of delivering, which anyone may do). Each planet circling a step is one transfer waiting at it. Each planet is its token's icon; its size is the value it carries. New transfers emerge from their source chain. Steps complete in batches, as on chain: a finalized checkpoint moves every transfer sent before it, one proof makes every finalized transfer of its direction ready, and deliveries come several at a time; each batch glides as a wave. At rest each step's planets crowd into a tilted orbit, seen in depth. Hover a step to open its orbit; hover a planet (tap on a phone) to stop it and read the transfer; hover a step's sun for what waits there, by token, with its fees and what delivering it all would earn; hover a chain for its totals.

  • Orbits use the design language's one ring radius, multiplied: the first orbit holds 6 planets, each further orbit 6 more. Every planet eases toward its place, so moves, arrivals and re-spacing glide.
  • Delivered transfers leave through the right end and the count above ticks up.
  • With reduced motion, dots sit still on their rings; hover and tap work the same.
