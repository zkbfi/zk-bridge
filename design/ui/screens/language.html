Our design language, and the card built from it

Everything in the mark is one of five shapes. A UI built from the same five adds no new curve. The open question is the panel corner: square (B-cut like every stroke end) or a full pill.

Straight 3-unit stroke, ends cut square (the deck, stems). Rules, dividers, borders.
The ring, one radius only. Loaders and progress, never decoration.
Heavy dots: source (solid) and destination (faint until reached). Chain ends, status.
The pill (the pulse): the only rounded rectangle. Buttons, chips, fields, the thing in flight.
50.4° diagonal (z and k arm, parallel). Direction: the "→" between chains.
A

Square panels, pill controls

Panels and fields are cut square with a 3-unit rule, like the deck. Every control is a pill. Two shapes, clearly separated: structure is square, things you touch are pills.
